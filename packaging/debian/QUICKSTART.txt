TardiSHA v23 Grimchain Quick Start

Basic use:
  grimchain path/to/file
  grimchain path/to/directory

Synodic Magicae center:
  grimchain 64 path/to/file
  grimchain 64 path/to/directory

Save and verify:
  grimchain 1000000 source.bin --output source.grim
  grimchain source.bin --verify --output source.grim

Help:
  grimchain --help
  grimchain --help -a
